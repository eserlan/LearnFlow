# How will climate change impact agriculture?

This short video provides a global perspective on agriculture in a changing climate.

Watch this video (5:12 min.) from American space agency NASA's series called "Science for a Hungry World". It uses a number of difficult words, so remember to activate the English subtitles and take note of any new words to look up in a dictionary afterwards.

## Questions:

- Give 2-3 specific examples of how the climate can affect agriculture.

- "Vulnerability to climate is uneven across the world." Can you explain what this quotation from the video means?

- How does population growth affect agriculture?

- If you had the power to change the world's global response to climate change and the impact on agriculture, what would you do?
